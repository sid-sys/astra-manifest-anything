# Maya — what the app is

## In one line

Maya is a mobile app (Android) that turns a person's wish into a short guided
audio they can listen to again and again, plus five affirmations they can say
and believe. Built for a US audience.

## The idea

People who manifest usually have a wish ("I want to buy my dream home") but no
easy way to practise it every day. Maya takes the wish, asks four quick
questions about it, then writes and reads a personal meditation and a set of
affirmations. The person listens, repeats, and gets gentle reminders during the
day.

## What a person can do

- **Write a wish** and get a **manifestation**: a calm, personal audio in a
  human-sounding voice with soft background music. Replay it as often as they
  like.
- **Get affirmations**: five short lines about their wish. Free for everybody.
  They can also be listened to as audio, on a loop, with background music.
- **Get reminders**: morning and evening notifications that carry their own
  affirmation lines.
- **Keep everything** in a library and delete anything they no longer want.
- **Subscribe** to Maya Plus for a monthly number of manifestations. New
  subscribers get a free trial, priced in US dollars.

## The screens

**Sign in**
Email and password, Google, or continue as a guest. New email accounts confirm
their address first. There is a forgot-password screen too.

**Home**
The main screen. A box that asks what the person is working towards. Pressing
Continue starts the flow. Also shows recent manifestations and how many are left
this month.

**The four questions**
After typing a wish, Maya asks four multiple-choice questions written for that
exact wish (for example about the place, the feeling, who is there). The answers
shape the writing.

**Generating**
A calm waiting screen while Maya writes and records. Affirmations are written
first, then the manifestation.

**Paywall (Maya Plus)**
Shows monthly and yearly plans, the free trial, samples of how Maya writes, and
a Restore purchase link. Non-subscribers see it when they try to make a
manifestation.

**Player**
Plays a manifestation or an affirmation set. Play and pause, back 10 seconds,
forward 30 seconds, speed, **loop**, and **background music** with its own
volume. Words show on screen to read along. Favourite and report buttons.

**Library**
Everything Maya has written, newest first, filterable. Each item can be played,
queued or deleted (with a confirmation).

**Affirm**
Type a goal, answer the same four questions, get five lines. Past sets are
listed below. Each set can be played as a looping audio or deleted.

**Profile**
Name, photo, life areas, reminder times, this month's usage, saved items,
subscription, legal links, sign out and delete account.

**Admin panel** (only for the owner)
- Everyone who uses the app: name, email, wish, how many manifestations and
  affirmations they made.
- Where each person stopped: opened the app, typed a wish, saw the price,
  pressed subscribe, paid. Shows how many times, whether the free trial was
  visible, and the last purchase error.
- Grant or remove a trial for a person, close an account.
- **Announcements**: send a message to every phone and see who opened it.
- Remove old onboarding fields from all accounts.

|---|---|---|
| Affirmations (text and audio) | Yes | Yes |
| Reminders with own affirmations | Yes | Yes |
| Manifestations (audio) | No | Monthly allowance |
| Replay everything already made | Yes | Yes |
| Free trial | | Yes, 3 days |

## Things to know

- All prices are in US dollars. Google Play shows the local currency
  automatically to people in other countries.
- Old onboarding questions no longer exist. Only the name, life areas,
  reminder settings and subscription status are kept on a person's profile.
- People can delete their own manifestations, affirmations and account.
