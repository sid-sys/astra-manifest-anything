# Creator partner: ad, application form, agreement outline

Plain words on purpose. Not legal advice: have a lawyer read the agreement
before anyone signs.

---

## 1. The ad (Meta, Instagram, X, Reddit r/forhire)

**Headline:** Own part of a wellness app. Make videos. Share the profit.

**Text:**

I built Mayow, an app that turns your wish into a personal audio
manifestation, read to you in a calm voice. The app is finished and live on
Google Play, with a free trial and a paid plan.

I can build and fix. I cannot make people care. I am looking for one short-video
creator (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) to be my partner.

What you get:
- 50% of the net profit of the app (after store fees and running costs)
- Your share is earned over 4 years, so you grow into it
- Your name on the brand, and your own tracking link so you see your results

What you do:
- Make and post 1 to 2 short videos a day
- Test what works and repeat it
- Check your numbers every week with me

What I need from you:
- 3 videos of yours that got real views
- An audience that likes wellness, self-growth, sleep or journaling
- You can start now and keep going for at least 90 days

I cannot pay a salary right now. This is ownership. If you want a paid gig,
this is not it. If you want to build something with me, send the form.

Apply: [link to form]

## 2. Application form (keep it short)

1. Your name and where you live
2. Your TikTok / Instagram / YouTube links
3. Links to your 3 best videos, and how many views each got
4. Who watches you? (age, country, what they like)
5. How many videos can you post each day?
6. Have you made UGC or app videos before? Send a link.
7. Download Mayow and make one 30-second video about it. Send the link.
8. Can you work on this for at least 90 days? (Yes / No)
9. Why do you want a share instead of a fixed pay?

**How to choose:** read answers 3, 4 and 7 first. Ignore follower count. Pick
2 or 3 people for the trial. Keep the one who brings the most real trial
sign-ups.

## 3. Agreement outline (one page, both sign)

**Parties:** [You] and [Creator]. Project: Mayow, the Android app and the
brand.

1. **Roles.**
   - You: build the app, fix bugs, manage payments and servers, show the
     numbers every week.
   - Creator: ideas, filming, editing, posting, replying to comments, weekly
     check-in. Minimum 1 video a day on average.
2. **Trial: first 90 days.** Either person can stop in the first 90 days with 7
   days' notice. After that, the sections below apply.
3. **Profit.** "Net profit" means Mayow's money in each month, minus Google
   Play's fee, voice and writing costs, server and tool costs, and taxes.
   Split 50 / 50. Paid monthly, with the numbers shown.
4. **Earning your share.** The creator's 50% is earned over 4 years. Nothing is
   earned in the first 12 months. After 12 months, 25% of the share is earned,
   then a little each month. If the creator leaves early, they keep only what
   they have earned.
5. **Who owns what.**
   - The app, its code, the name Mayow and every brand account (TikTok,
     Instagram, YouTube, email) belong to the company. You keep all the
     logins.
   - Videos made for the brand belong to the brand. The creator may keep
     posting them on their own account.
   - The creator's own personal account stays theirs.
6. **Content rules.** No false claims. Mayow does not promise money, love or
   health results. No medical advice. Say "ad" or "partner" where required by
   the platform.
7. **Tracking.** Each creator gets a link or code. Counted: installs, free
   trials, paying users. Both can see the same numbers.
8. **Leaving.** What happens to accounts, passwords and unpaid share is written
   here. The creator hands over every login on leaving.
9. **Keeping quiet.** Neither shares private numbers, code or logins.
10. **Disagreements.** Talk first. If still stuck, [agreed person or service]
    decides.

**Before signing:** a lawyer reads it, and you both read it twice.
